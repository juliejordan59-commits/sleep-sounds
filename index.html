📱 iPhone: if you hear nothing, check the silent switch on the side (it can mute web sounds) and turn up the phone volume. Keep this page open; you can let the screen dim. For best results tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”.

The sleep timer fades out gently over the last minute.

🌙 Night schedule: up to 3 sessions a night, each fading in over about 45 seconds and out over the last minute. Times after midnight count as later tonight. Starting the sleep timer cancels the schedule, and starting the schedule turns the sleep timer off.

For the schedule to work on iPhone: leave this app open (don’t swipe it away), plug in your phone, keep the silent switch off and the volume up. You can lock the screen. Between sessions the app keeps playing silence in the background so later sessions can start on time. Honest caveat: iOS may still stop it in rare cases (a phone call, an alarm, another app playing audio, Low Power Mode, or iOS closing the app to free memory). If that happens, open the app and tap “Start night schedule” again.
